FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our collections, select your size and add items to your bag. Proceed to checkout and complete your payment securely via Stripe.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe — Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be amended or cancelled within 24 hours of placement. Contact us at support@noir-store.com as soon as possible.

How do I find my size?

Our garments run true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up. Contact us if you need specific measurements.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to most countries worldwide. Delivery times and costs vary by destination — see our Shipping page for details.
